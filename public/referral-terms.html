Refer & Reward terms

Pilot programme. Last updated: 24 September 2026

These terms cover the Qura Refer & Reward pilot, run by Qura Ltd, trading as Qura Health ("Qura", "we", "us"), registered in England & Wales (company no. 17310951), 167-169 Great Portland Street, London W1W 5PF. They sit alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

How it works

If you have a Qura account as a healthcare professional, you have a personal invite link and a 6-character invite code. Share them with healthcare professionals you know. When someone joins Qura through your link, or enters your code when they create their account, the referral is recorded against you.

What counts as a successful referral

A referral is successful, and earns a reward, once all of the following are true:

  • the person you referred is a healthcare professional who did not already have a Qura account;
  • they created their account through your link, or entered your code within 14 days of creating it;
  • they have confirmed their email address;
  • they have completed their professional profile, and Qura has verified it.

Clicks on your link, accounts that are started but not completed, and profiles we cannot verify do not count.

The reward

For each successful referral you receive a £10 digital gift voucher. During this pilot you can earn rewards for up to 10 successful referrals, a maximum of £100 in total. Further referrals are still welcome and are still recorded, without a reward.

Vouchers are issued by hand by the Qura team and sent to the email address on your Qura account, normally within 30 days of the referral becoming successful. We choose the voucher provider. There is no cash alternative, and vouchers cannot be transferred or exchanged.

What is not allowed

  • Referring yourself, including through a second account or a second email address.
  • Creating accounts that are duplicate, fake, or that do not belong to a real healthcare professional.
  • Using someone else's registration details.
  • Sending your link as unsolicited bulk messages (spam), or presenting yourself as speaking for Qura.
  • Offering to share your reward with the person you refer in exchange for them joining.

We may decline a referral, withhold or reclaim a reward, or remove someone from the programme where we reasonably believe these terms have been broken. Our records of who referred whom decide any question about attribution.

Who can take part

You must be 18 or over and hold a Qura account as a healthcare professional. Qura staff and founders cannot earn rewards. We may limit the pilot to particular countries or professions, and will say so on this page if we do.

Your information

We record which account referred which, and the status of each referral. If you referred someone, you see how many of your referrals are pending, successful or rewarded. You do not see their profile through the programme. We use this information to run the programme and to measure whether it works, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Changes and ending the pilot

This is a pilot. We may change these terms, the reward or the cap, or end the programme, by updating this page. Any referral that became successful before a change or before the programme ends will be rewarded on the terms that applied at that time.

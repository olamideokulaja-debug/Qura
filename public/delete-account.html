Delete your Qura account

Last updated: 27 July 2026

You can delete your Qura account and the data held against it at any time. There are two ways to do this.

In the app

Open the Qura app, go to Account, and choose "Delete my account". You will be asked to confirm. Deletion begins immediately and you are signed out.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email support@qurahealth.org from the address on your Qura account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in credentials, your name and email address, your professional profile including profession, registration details, country and experience, any CV or professional summary you uploaded, and your activity on the platform such as applications, saved opportunities and introduction requests.

What is kept, and why

We keep records of completed payments and invoices for as long as UK tax and accounting law requires. These are held by us and by our payment provider, Stripe, and are not used to contact you or to rebuild your profile. Where a supplier has already received an introduction to you, the record that the introduction took place is retained for our own contractual and dispute-handling purposes.

Questions

For anything relating to your data, email privacy@qurahealth.org. Our full privacy policy explains what we collect and your other rights.

How Qura sources SEND data

Last updated 8 October 2026

SEND Intelligence is a research service for specialist staffing and therapy suppliers. It brings together public information about special schools, alternative provision, schools with SEN units, the trusts and councils that run them, the vacancies they advertise and related public tenders, so that suppliers can find and respond to staffing needs sooner.

Where the information comes from

  • Official school records: Get Information about Schools (Department for Education) for England; School contact details (Scottish Government); Address list of schools (Welsh Government); School enrolment school-level data (Department of Education, Northern Ireland). Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. The Scottish Government asks commercial callers to seek permission from the local authority before contacting schools directly.
  • Vacancies: the careers or vacancies pages that schools, academy trusts and councils publish on their own websites, checked about once a day. Each vacancy links back to the original advert, and applications are always made through the employer's own process.
  • Tenders: public procurement notices from Find a Tender and Contracts Finder, including pipeline and market engagement notices and the end dates of contracts already let, and the below-threshold notices published on Public Contracts Scotland and Sell2Wales (Sell2Wales once its data service is working again).
  • Council figures: Department for Education statistics on education, health and care plans; Ofsted management information on school inspections; Ofsted and Care Quality Commission area SEND inspection outcomes; council forward plans of key decisions published on council websites (Qura reads only sites whose robots.txt allows it and that serve pages without a bot check, keeps the item title, decision maker role, due date and link, and never stores officer names); Education and Skills Funding Agency dedicated schools grant allocations; and Department for Education publications on Safety Valve agreements and the Delivering Better Value in SEND programme. All published under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

We do not use commercial job boards unless we hold a licence to do so, and we do not use Teaching Vacancies unless the Department for Education agrees.

Children and families

Qura never collects, stores or shows any information about pupils, families or Education, Health and Care Plans. Where we show figures such as the number of pupils with an EHC plan at a school, these are totals published in official school records.

Information about staff

SEND Intelligence does not currently show the names or contact details of school staff. If we add them, we will show only names, roles and work contact details that schools, trusts and councils publish themselves, with a link to where we found them and the date we last checked, and we will update this page first. We never guess or invent email addresses. You can use the form below at any time to ask us not to show your details.

QuraBot

Our checks identify themselves as QuraBot/1.0. QuraBot follows each site's robots.txt, makes at most a few requests to a site each day, never signs in, and never gets around paywalls or bot protection. To stop QuraBot visiting your site, add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: QuraBot
Disallow: /

Ask us to remove your details

We stop showing matching details straight away and confirm by email within one month if you give an address.
